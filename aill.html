01About Us

From Port to Border. From Border to Port. One Integrated Logistics Partner.

African Inland Logistics Limited (AILL), a flagship company of Lake Group, provides integrated cargo-handling and logistics solutions across port handling, warehousing, bagging, customs clearance, road and rail transport for bulk, break-bulk and containerized cargo.

AILL coordinates the cargo journey from vessel discharge and port handling through clearance, transport, storage, processing and final border or port delivery. Strategically located facilities, dedicated equipment, rail capability and experienced teams support the safe, efficient movement of high-volume cargo across the region.

Integrated Logistics. Operational Excellence. Regional Connectivity. AILL and its Group company's ICD/CFS operations extend across Tanzania, Zambia and Mozambique, supported by Lake Group's wider regional network.

  • Port handling and vessel-to-inland coordination
  • Warehousing and secure open-yard storage
  • Bagging, stuffing and de-stuffing operations
  • Customs clearance and compliance support
  • Road and rail transportation
  • Bulk, break-bulk and containerized cargo (stuffing & de-stuffing)
  • Sulphur, copper, lithium and other bulk & bagged products
