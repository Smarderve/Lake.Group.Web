Lake Trans Ltd.

Lake Trans

Lake Group's logistics arm, established in 2008 and operating a fleet of 650 vehicles across East and Central Africa.

01Company Introduction

Value Services to Clients

Lake Trans Ltd., the second company of Lake Group, was established in 2008 as the Group's logistics arm, specialising in local and transit transportation of petroleum products.

The company combines trained drivers and staff with modern vehicle tracking to support safe cargo movement and efficient delivery to internationally recognised standards. Lake Trans is a member of the Tanzania Truck Owners Association (TATOA).

  • Local and transit petroleum transportation
  • Tracking devices fitted across the fleet
  • Skilled drivers, mechanics and engineers
  • Branches in Congo, Burundi and Zambia
  • Fuel transport to Uganda and Rwanda

04Fleet & Operations

Built for Regional Haulage

The 650-vehicle fleet includes petroleum and LPG tankers, tippers, premix concrete mixers, auxiliary trucks and flatbeds.

Carrier and petroleum trucks provide capacities from 12,000 to 40,000 litres. Fleet brands named in the company profile include Scania, Iveco, Sinotruk and Dayun, supported by trailers from established regional suppliers.

  • 100 tankers assigned to local operations
  • 250 tankers assigned to transit operations
  • Vehicle tracking after border crossings
  • Workshops in Monduli, Dar es Salaam and Morogoro
  • One yard at Kibaha and two at Kigamboni
05Fleet Highlights

Profile Milestones

The supplied company profile records the import and regional deployment of 300 Sinotruk vehicles, followed by a programme to bring an additional 150 trucks into Tanzania by the end of November 2017.

It also reports that Lake Trans transported 200 million litres of fuel and covered 10 million kilometres in 2016. These are historical milestones from the profile, not current-year performance figures.
