01Company Introduction

Vehicle & Trailer Assembly Solutions

ATL Limited (Assembly Tech Ltd), established in 2019, has evolved from aluminium trailer manufacturing into a broader vehicle and trailer assembly business. The company provides quality, safety and durability-focused solutions for transportation, logistics and specialized operating requirements across East and Central Africa.

06Company History

From Aluminum Trailers Limited To Assembly Tech Limited

Established in 2019 as Aluminum Trailers Limited, the company rebranded as Assembly Tech Ltd in 2026 as part of its continued development and expansion of premium customized trailer solutions.
