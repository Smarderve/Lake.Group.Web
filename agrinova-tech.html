About Agrinova

Transforming agriculture.
Empowering farmers.

Agrinova is an agricultural transformation and solutions company focused on empowering farmers through reliable machinery and practical solutions for modern agricultural production across Tanzania.

From land preparation and planting to crop management, irrigation, harvesting, threshing and transportation, Agrinova supports the agricultural cycle with one connected offering.

Vision

To be the catalyst for mechanized agriculture and one of the top five market leaders in the agricultural sector in Africa.

Mission

To drive agricultural transformation across Tanzania by providing accessible, reliable and innovative machinery solutions.

FAQ

Questions about Agrinova

What agricultural machinery does Agrinova provide?

Tractors, tillage equipment, planting equipment, crop management tools, irrigation systems, harvesting, threshing and trailers.

What tractor brands and horsepower ranges are available?

Eicher tractors are available from 45–85 HP and YTO tractors from 55–240 HP.

Does Agrinova provide spare parts and after sales support?

Agrinova provides genuine spare parts, after sales service, warranty support, technical assistance, maintenance support and customer guidance.

Where is Agrinova located?

The head office and showroom are on Pugu Road opposite JNIA Terminal 3 in Dar es Salaam. Factory and support are in Kibaha, Tanzania.

Can I request machinery based on my farm requirements?

Contact Agrinova to discuss your farm, equipment requirements and available financing options.
