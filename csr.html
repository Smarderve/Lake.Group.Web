CSR & Sustainability

Growing Communities, Sustaining Futures.

Lake Group embeds sustainability and corporate social responsibility in its business philosophy across Africa and the Middle East.

Responsible growth, shared value.

Our commitment is to create positive economic, social, moral and environmental impact within the communities we serve.

We believe responsible growth is sustainable, accountable and transparent, creating shared value for all stakeholders.

Through our CSR initiatives, we support practical programmes that help improve livelihoods and contribute to long-term progress.

CSR focus areas

Education

Support for education that helps communities build opportunity.

Health

Support for health and wellbeing in the communities we serve.

Community development

Practical support for stronger, more resilient communities.

Employment creation

Creating opportunities that help improve livelihoods.

Featured impact

Lake Gas clean energy access programme

Through collaboration with the Rural Energy Agency (REA) and local governments, Lake Gas has enabled more than 150,000 rural residents to adopt clean cooking energy.

Sustainability in our operations

We voluntarily integrate social and environmental responsibility into our business operations, continuously seeking ways to address community needs while contributing to sustainable development.

By combining business success with meaningful impact, we strive to build stronger communities and a more sustainable future.
