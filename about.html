01Our Story

From a Single Fuel Station to the Leading Powerhouse

Lake Group, established in 2006 with a single fuel outlet in Dar es Salaam, Tanzania, has now grown to a whole regional business group.

Its fast growing energy trading, logistics, manufacturing, and automotive industries, have now marked its presence through East and Central Africa, and the Middle East. Today, Lake Group has its presence globally across 10 countries.

Keeping diversity and inclusion in the workforce, the group consists of 30,000+ people from 10 nationalities, continuing to grow with a purpose.
