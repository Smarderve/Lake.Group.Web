Lake Agro · Rufiji, Tanzania

Lake Agro

Developing an integrated sugarcane plantation and sugar manufacturing project in Rufiji, Tanzania.

01Company Introduction

Integrated sugarcane and sugar manufacturing.

Lake Agro Limited is a Tanzania-based Lake Group company established in 2021. It is developing an integrated large-scale sugarcane plantation and sugar manufacturing plant on 16,000 hectares of land.

The current plant capacity is 2,500 TCD and is expandable to 3,500 TCD. Bosch Projects (Pty) Ltd of South Africa and Booker Tate Limited of the United Kingdom are the international consultants supporting the project.

03Plantation Development

Growing with efficient irrigation.

634 hectares of sugarcane have been planted using centre pivots, semi-solid and drip irrigation systems.

The integrated development combines plantation operations with sugar manufacturing, supported by sustainable agricultural and manufacturing practices.
