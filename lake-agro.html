01Company Introduction

Developing a modern, integrated and commercially sustainable agricultural business.

Lake Agro Ltd is a Lake Group agricultural company established in Zambia in 2017. The company focuses on sustainable agricultural growth through modern farming, strengthening food security through large-scale and reliable food production, and integrating crop and livestock production to build a diversified and resilient agribusiness driven by technology and innovation.

In addition to its Zambia commercial farming operations, Lake Agro is developing an integrated sugarcane plantation and sugar manufacturing project in Tanzania, initiated in 2021.

06Tanzania Sugarcane & Sugar Project

Growing with efficient irrigation.

Lake Agro initiated its integrated sugarcane and sugar manufacturing project in Tanzania in 2021, associated with 16,000 hectares of land.

634 hectares of sugarcane have been planted using Centre Pivot, Semi-solid and Drip irrigation systems. The development is supported by 17 km of 33KV High Tension Power Line and further bulk-water infrastructure.

The current plant capacity is 2,500 TCD and is expandable to 3,500 TCD. Bosch Projects (Pty) Ltd of South Africa and Booker Tate Limited of the United Kingdom are the international consultants supporting the project.
