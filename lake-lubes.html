Lake Lubes Ltd. · Est. 2016

Lake Lubes

Specialized lubricant and grease manufacturing for Tanzania and the wider East African market.

01Company Introduction

Engineered for Performance

Lake Lubes Ltd is a specialized lubricant and grease manufacturing company and part of the Lake Group, established in 2016 to support the growing demand for high-quality automotive, industrial and specialty lubricants in Tanzania and the wider East African market.

Lake Lubes operates a fully integrated Lube Oil Blending Plant (LOBP), supported by modern blending technology, strict quality control systems and experienced technical personnel.

  • 4.2 million litres of base-oil storage capacity
  • 10 blenders with combined capacity of 54,000 litres per day
  • Dedicated blender for coolant manufacturing
  • In-house jerry cans from 250 ml to 20 litres
  • In-house laboratory quality control across blending, filling and packaging

02About the Company

Mission, Vision & Core Values

Mission

To deliver safe, reliable and high-quality lubricants and greases to every sector in Tanzania through self-reliant manufacturing, rigorous quality control and a responsive nationwide distribution network, supporting a more industrialized and efficient Tanzania.

Vision

To become East Africa's leading and most trusted lubricant manufacturer, setting high standards in quality, innovation and self-sufficiency while delivering total customer satisfaction and contributing to the region's industrial and economic growth.

Quality Excellence

Committed to producing reliable lubricants and greases that meet required quality and performance standards.

Operational Excellence

Vigilant on efficiency, productivity and continuous improvement across our plant and supply chain.

Self-Reliance

Focused on in-house capability, from base oil handling to packaging manufacturing, to control quality at every stage.

National Development

Support Tanzania's industrial and economic growth through local manufacturing, employment and energy development.

Company Objectives

  • Maintain high quality and safety standards throughout blending, filling and packaging, backed by our in-house laboratory
  • Strengthen self-reliant packaging, supplying jerry cans from 250 ml to 20 litres without third-party dependence
  • Build a strong regional presence across East Africa, leveraging the Lake Group network
  • Develop our workforce through technical training, skills development and a strong quality and safety culture
03Products & Support

Product Categories & Support

Automotive Lubricants

Engine oils and lubricants for passenger and commercial vehicles.

Industrial Lubricants

Formulations for industrial and specialty applications.

Greases

Manufactured to meet automotive and industrial requirements.

Coolants

Manufactured on a dedicated, separate blending line.

Base Oil Handling

Dedicated pump house for base-oil offloading and 4.2 million litres of base-oil storage capacity.

In-House Packaging

Jerry can production from 250 ml to 20 litres; bulk drums of 200 litres and 180 kg.

Quality Testing & Inspection

Strict quality control across blending, filling and packaging through our in-house laboratory.

Nationwide Distribution

Supply and delivery through our own fleet of trucks.
