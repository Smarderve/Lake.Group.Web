01 Company Introduction

Commercial Mobility Solutions

NEXTDRIVE MOTORS is a commercial vehicle company specializing in the sales, distribution and support of reliable transportation solutions for businesses and individuals. Its portfolio includes JAC pick-ups, Ashok Leyland light and medium-duty trucks, SANY heavy commercial trucks and trailers designed for modern transportation and logistics requirements.
